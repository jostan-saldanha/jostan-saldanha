### 👨‍💻 Engineering Profile

- ⚡ **Embedded & Firmware Engineering:** Developing robust, fault-tolerant bare-metal and RTOS-based firmware for ARM Cortex-M (STM32, Silicon Labs EFR32/MGM260P, NXP) and Espressif microcontrollers.
- 🧠 **Edge AI & TinyML:** Implementing on-device machine learning models, IMU sensor fusion, and intelligent edge signal processing.
- ⌚ **Wearables & IoT Devices:** Designing custom sensor nodes, wearable health monitors, and low-power telemetry devices with dedicated energy profiling.
- 📡 **Wireless & Protocols:** Architecting resilient dual-path telemetry systems integrating **BLE GATT, MQTT, UART, I2C, SPI, and Wi-Fi**.
